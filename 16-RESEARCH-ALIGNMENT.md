# Research / TA / Journal Alignment

## Research problem
Seller operations can suffer from fragmented order, inventory and fulfillment information, making it difficult to determine which orders require immediate processing and whether those orders can actually be fulfilled.

## Proposed research contribution
Develop an integrated e-commerce fulfillment information system that:
1. synchronizes marketplace order information into an internal domain model;
2. validates stock availability;
3. reserves inventory;
4. calculates explainable fulfillment priority using configurable criteria;
5. validates picking/packing through scanning;
6. records shipment and return events;
7. measures operational impact.

## Candidate research variable
Independent/system intervention:
Integrated fulfillment management system with priority mechanism.

Possible dependent variables:
- order identification time
- priority determination time
- stock verification time
- picking error rate
- task completion time
- usability score

## Novelty guard
Do not claim:
"API integration is novel."

Do not claim:
"Inventory CRUD is novel."

Potential contribution:
"An integrated, configurable and explainable fulfillment prioritization workflow connecting marketplace order data, inventory availability and warehouse execution."

## Research questions
RQ1: How can marketplace order data and internal inventory data be integrated into a unified fulfillment workflow?
RQ2: How can configurable criteria be used to prioritize fulfillment tasks?
RQ3: Does the proposed system reduce the time required to identify and process priority orders in the selected case study?
RQ4: How usable is the system for operational users?

## Evaluation
Use a defined scenario set and real/representative operational data.
Document:
- sample size
- task definitions
- baseline procedure
- system procedure
- measurement method
- limitations

## Important academic discipline
The application is an artifact.
The research must explain:
- why the problem matters;
- what gap exists;
- what design decision addresses it;
- how the artifact was evaluated;
- what evidence supports the conclusions.
